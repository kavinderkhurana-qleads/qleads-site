Top up your wallet

For brokers registered with qleads.ai. Each Q-Lead is AED 500, debited from your prepaid wallet when the lead is delivered (prices exclude VAT). You are eligible for leads while your wallet holds AED 500.

Your first top-up: Your first top-up of AED 500 gets you 2 leads: the first is free, the second is paid from your AED 500. Not as described? Dispute within 24 hours and it is replaced free. For more leads, top up in multiples of AED 500.

The first AED 500 is a 2-lead starter pack: once your free lead has been delivered, that AED 500 is not refunded if you close your account. Any balance above it stays refundable, and credits never expire.
