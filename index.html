Questions

Questions about Qleads

I don't trust AI talking to my buyers.

It follows your rules, never invents prices, and hands the buyer to you or your team whenever he asks. You see every chat.

Do I keep my data if I stop?

Yes. Your buyers and chats belong to you.

Do I have to change my CRM?

No. Professional sends every enquiry to your CRM. On other plans you can export them.

What is the difference between a Q-Lead and a Q-Buyer?

A Q-Lead is qualified by AI in chat and sent to you instantly. A Q-Buyer has also confirmed five key points and been verified on a recorded call.

Are they exclusive?

Yes. Each Q-Lead and Q-Buyer is delivered to one customer only. The terms for your account give the details.

Is the first Q-Lead free?

Yes. Your first wallet top-up of AED 500 brings you one Q-Lead free of charge, delivered on your first Q-Lead match. This is given once per account.

What if a buyer is not right?

Tap Raise dispute on the delivery message within 24 hours of delivery. A Q-Lead that is not a genuine property buyer, or a Q-Buyer that is not as described, is replaced at no charge.

Where do the buyers come from?

Our own advertising in the countries you choose. Every buyer contacted us first.

I'm a single broker. Is this for me?

Yes. Start with Starter, or simply buy Q-Leads and Q-Buyers.

Is it hard to run?

No. We set it up and look after it.

How does the free trial work?

You sign a short agreement, then we set up your AI agent. Set-up can take up to 10 days. The 7 free days start when your agent goes live. All charges are on us and no card is asked for. At the end of day 7 the agent pauses unless you confirm your plan and pay.
