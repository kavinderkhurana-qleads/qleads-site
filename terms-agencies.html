4.4 All prices on qleads.ai exclude VAT where applicable.

5. Buyers we bring you

5.1 Q-Lead. A Q-Lead is an introduction to a property buyer who has been qualified by our AI in a WhatsApp chat and has consented to be contacted by one specialist. A Q-Lead is sent to you automatically. It is not voice-verified.

5.2 Q-Buyer. A Q-Buyer is an introduction to a property buyer who meets the following standard. The buyer has stated a genuine intention to purchase. The buyer has also confirmed their budget, area/locality preference, purpose of investment, type of property (off-plan or ready) and the purchase timeframe in the conversation as well as on the verification call before the Q-Buyer is delivered. A Q-Buyer is not delivered where the buyer has scored less than 80 out of 100 based on these parameters. The verification call is recorded with the buyer's consent, and the buyer has consented to be contacted by one specialist. A Q-Buyer is matched and handed over to you by our team after the verification call.

6. Wallet and delivery

6.1 Q-Leads and Q-Buyers are separate from the plan fee. They are paid from one prepaid wallet held by your agency.

6.2 Your first top-up is AED 500. After that you may top up any amount in steps of AED 100. Credits never expire. One card top-up is at most AED 25,000; larger amounts are paid by bank transfer and credited when the transfer arrives.

6.3 Each introduction is delivered on WhatsApp to one of the staff numbers you declare. You may change the numbers and the team members by written notice to support@qleads.ai, and the change is made within 24 hours.

6.4 You choose which products you receive and may set a monthly cap. Each delivery debits its price from your wallet. A product is delivered only while your wallet holds its price.

6.5 Your first top-up of AED 500 brings you one Q-Lead free of charge, delivered on your first Q-Lead match. This is given once per account. If you close your account after your free Q-Lead has been delivered, the first AED 500 is not refunded; any balance above it is refunded as set out in 6.6.

6.6 If you close your account, your wallet balance is refunded. Card refunds take 5 to 10 business days. For money paid by bank transfer, we confirm when the refund is sent.

8. Disputes and replacement

8.1 Q-Buyer. If a delivered Q-Buyer does not meet the standard in Section 5.2, raise a dispute within 24 hours of delivery by tapping Raise dispute on the delivery message in WhatsApp. The Lead ID printed on every delivery message identifies the introduction. A valid dispute is resolved by a replacement Q-Buyer at no charge.

8.2 On a dispute, we provide you with the chat history and the recording of the verification call for that Q-Buyer. You use them only for the dispute and treat them as confidential buyer personal data.

8.3 Q-Lead. If a Q-Lead is not a genuine property buyer, for example the buyer says he never asked to be contacted or is himself an agent, raise a dispute within 24 hours of delivery by tapping Raise dispute. A valid dispute is resolved by a replacement Q-Lead at no charge.
