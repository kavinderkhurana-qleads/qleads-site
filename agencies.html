Your own AI agent, and buyers for your team.

Qleads sets up an AI agent in your agency's name on your own WhatsApp number. It answers every buyer, qualifies him and hands him to the right member of your team. We also deliver buyers from our own campaigns.

What you get

What a brokerage agency gets from Qleads

Your own AI agent

It runs on your own WhatsApp number, in your agency's name. It answers every buyer in the buyer's own language. It asks budget, area, purpose, property type and timing, then hands the buyer to the right member of your team.

Buyers delivered to you

Q-Leads are buyers qualified by AI in a WhatsApp chat, AED 100 each. Q-Buyers are also verified on a recorded call, AED 500 each. Each is delivered to your agency only. Your first wallet top-up of AED 500 brings you one Q-Lead free of charge.

Custom AI work

Marketing campaigns, AI emailing, AI voice calling, CRM integration and dashboards, listings and content, website chat and other channels. Priced to your needs. Request a quote

7-day free trial

You sign a short agreement, then we set up your WhatsApp number and AI agent. Set-up can take up to 3 days. Your 7 free days start when your AI agent goes live. No card.
