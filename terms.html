1. Who can register

qleads.ai is for real-estate brokers. You confirm your registration details are accurate at sign-up. Registration is completed by replying "I AGREE" to the confirmation summary on WhatsApp, which constitutes your acceptance of these terms and a valid electronic signature under UAE law.

2. What a lead is

A "Q-Buyer" is an introduction to a property buyer who has been qualified by AiSHA, voice-verified, and has consented to be contacted by one broker. qleads.ai provides the introduction; it is not a party to any transaction between you and the buyer and is not a brokerage.

3. Pricing

  • Leads are AED 500 each, excluding VAT where applicable.
  • Registration is free. You become eligible for leads once your wallet holds AED 500.
  • Paid leads are drawn from a prepaid wallet topped up in steps of AED 500. There is no mandatory recharge, and credits never expire.
  • Your first top-up of AED 500 is a starter pack of 2 leads: the first lead is free and the second is paid from that AED 500.
  • One card top-up is at most AED 25,000. Larger amounts are paid by bank transfer and credited to your wallet when the transfer arrives.
  • If you close your account, your balance is refunded, except the AED 500 starter pack when your free lead has been delivered and no paid lead has been delivered since. Card refunds take 5 to 10 business days. For money paid by bank transfer, we confirm when the refund is sent.

4. Exclusivity

Each lead is delivered to one broker only and is never shared with another broker.

5. Disputes & replacement

If a delivered lead does not match what was promised, raise a dispute within 24 hours of delivery by tapping Raise dispute on the lead message in WhatsApp. The Lead ID printed on every lead message identifies the lead. Valid disputes are resolved by a replacement lead. The voice verification for each lead is recorded and held to resolve disputes fairly.

6. No guarantee of outcome

We qualify and verify every lead, but we do not guarantee that any lead will transact or convert. Conversion depends on your own follow-up and service.

7. Your responsibilities

  • Contact leads promptly and professionally;
  • Handle buyer data lawfully and only for the introduction;
  • Comply with RERA and all applicable UAE laws and advertising rules.

8. Limitation of liability

To the fullest extent permitted by law, our liability is limited to replacing a non-conforming lead (the sole and exclusive remedy, per section 5) or, on account closure, refunding your wallet balance as set out in section 3. Nothing excludes liability that cannot be excluded under UAE law.

9. Governing law

These terms are governed by the laws of the United Arab Emirates, with the courts of Dubai having jurisdiction.
