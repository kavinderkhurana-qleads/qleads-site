Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 November 2026

This policy explains how qleads.ai handles personal data. qleads.ai is operated by Middle East Integrated Technologies FZ-LLC (Registration No. 0000004093293; Licence No. 47035238), a Free Zone LLC registered in the Ras Al Khaimah Economic Zone (RAKEZ) at CWEP8800, Compass Building, Al Shohada Road, Al Hamra Industrial Zone-FZ, Ras Al Khaimah, United Arab Emirates ("Qleads", "we", "us").

It covers our three kinds of customer: individual brokers, brokerage agencies and real estate developers. It also says what we do with the data of the property buyers we introduce to them.

1. What we collect

From customers (brokers, agencies and developers)

  • Registration details you give us on WhatsApp: your name, WhatsApp number, RERA or BRN number, agency and the areas you cover.
  • Trial form and quote form details from this website: your name, WhatsApp number, email address, company, whether you are a broker, an agency or a developer, the plan you chose, and the services you ticked for a quote.
  • For agencies and developers: the details in your agreement, and the staff or sales team WhatsApp numbers you declare for deliveries.
  • Your WhatsApp chats with our AI advisor.
  • Wallet and payment records: your balance, top-ups, deliveries, packages, disputes and refunds. Card payments are taken by our card processor, Stripe. Your card details never touch our servers.
  • Standard technical data when you use this website.

From property buyers

  • The buyer's name, WhatsApp number and property requirements, and the chat with the AI advisor.
  • For a Q-Buyer: a recording of the verification call, made with the buyer's consent.

2. Why we use it

  • To create and run your account, and to check that you qualify for it.
  • To answer a trial or quote request and to set up your trial or plan.
  • To match and deliver Q-Leads and Q-Buyers to you.
  • To manage your wallet, billing and any dispute.
  • To operate and improve the service.

We use your details on these grounds: to provide the service you asked for or registered for; to meet our legal and accounting duties; and with your consent, where we ask for it. Where we rely on your consent, you may withdraw it at any time by writing to us.

3. Buyer data we share with you

When a Q-Lead or a Q-Buyer is delivered to you, we share that buyer's contact details and requirements with you only, so that you can follow up on the introduction. The buyer has agreed to this in the chat before anything is shared.

You must handle that data lawfully, use it only for the introduction, and comply with UAE data-protection law.

4. Who we share data with

  • Service providers. We use trusted service providers to run the service: the WhatsApp messaging platform, hosting and database, email, and card payments. They process data for us, not for themselves. WhatsApp messages are also processed by WhatsApp/Meta under their own privacy policy, which we do not control.
  • On a dispute only. If you raise a dispute on a Q-Buyer, we give you the chat history and the recording of the verification call for that buyer, as your terms state. They go to the one customer the buyer was introduced to, and to no one else. You use them only for the dispute and keep them confidential. A recording made before 24 November 2026 is not shared with anyone.
  • We never sell personal data.

5. How long we keep it

  • Customer data: while your account is active, and as needed for legal and accounting purposes.
  • Buyer chat transcripts: for as long as we are providing the service, and as the law requires.
  • Verification call recordings: approximately 90 days after the transaction or any dispute closes, then deleted.
  • Trial form and quote form details: 12 months from the date of the request, unless you become a customer.
  • Wallet and payment records: 5 years from the date of the payment, or longer where the law requires it.

You can ask us to close your account and delete your data.

6. Your rights

Under UAE data-protection law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) you may access, correct or delete your personal data and withdraw consent. Write to us at the address below.

7. Cookies

This website sets no cookies of its own and uses no tracking or advertising tools. When you pay by card, the payment page is run by our payment provider, which uses its own essential cookies to take the payment safely. If we ever use measurement cookies for adverts, a cookie banner will ask for your choice first.

8. Security

We use appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to keep your information safe.

9. Contact

Questions or requests about your data: support@qleads.ai.

10. Updates

We may update this policy from time to time. The date above shows the latest version.
